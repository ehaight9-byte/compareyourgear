Sweetwater vs. zZounds vs. Guitar Center vs. AMS: Which Financing Actually Saves You Money?

Quick verdict: If you want interest-free payments with no credit check, zZounds' Pay As You Play or AMS's EzPay are your best bets. If you want a store credit card with long 0% promo windows for bigger purchases, Sweetwater or Guitar Center/Musician's Friend can work — but only if you pay it off inside the promo window. Miss that window and you're looking at 34.99% APR, which turns a "0% deal" into one of the most expensive ways to buy gear.

The Two Categories of Financing

Before comparing specific retailers, it helps to know these plans split into two totally different products:

In-house, no-credit-check payment plans. zZounds Pay As You Play, AMS EzPay, and Sweetwater's 3 Easy Payments all work the same basic way: split your purchase into a handful of payments, no interest, no credit bureau involvement (for smaller amounts). These are short-term — a few months at most — and capped at how much you can finance without a credit check.

Store credit cards with promotional financing. Sweetwater Card, Guitar Center/Musician's Friend's Platinum Card — these are real credit cards, issued by Synchrony, that require a credit check and open a revolving account. They offer genuinely 0% interest for a set promo window (up to 48 months on qualifying purchases), which is powerful for a $3,000+ guitar or amp. The catch: if you don't pay off the entire balance before the promo period ends, deferred interest kicks in retroactively — and both cards currently carry a 34.99% purchase APR with a 39.99% penalty APR. That's not a typo, and it's the single most important thing to understand before signing up.

zZounds: Pay As You Play

zZounds' financing lets you split a purchase into 4, 6, 8, 12, or 18 monthly payments, interest-free, with no credit bureau reporting on amounts up to $1,000. Above that, they'll run a soft credit check that doesn't affect your score. It doesn't get much more musician-friendly than this — no store card, no revolving balance, just a fixed payment schedule tied to that specific order.

Best for: Pedals, mid-range guitars, anything under $1,000 you want to pay off fast without touching your credit.

American Musical Supply: EzPay

AMS offers 4, 6, 8, and 12-payment plans at 0% interest, billed directly (not through a third-party card) every 31 days after your order ships. The 4 and 6-pay tiers generally don't require a credit check; longer terms do. For bigger purchases (roughly $999–$17,500), AMS also partners with Affirm for an 18-month 0% APR plan, which does require a credit check.

Worth noting: AMS's customer reviews are mixed on customer service responsiveness compared to zZounds and Sweetwater, so it's worth reading current reviews on the retailer itself, separate from the financing terms.

Best for: Musicians who want a longer no-credit-check runway (up to 8 payments) than zZounds offers.

Sweetwater: 3 Easy Payments vs. the Sweetwater Card

Sweetwater's 3 Easy Payments plan is the most accessible option on this list — it kicks in on purchases as low as $45 and goes up to $4,000, split into 3 interest-free payments with no credit check. The tradeoff is the short 3-month window, meaning your monthly payment on a big-ticket item can get steep fast (a $4,000 guitar splits into $1,333/month).

The Sweetwater Card is where the long financing windows live — up to 48 months at 0% on qualifying brands and purchases. This is genuinely useful for a $5,000+ setup if you're disciplined about paying it off in full before the promo ends. If you're not, that 34.99% APR applies retroactively to the entire original balance, not just what's left.

Best for: Sub-$4,000 purchases via 3 Easy Payments (fast, no credit check); larger purchases via the card only if you're confident you'll clear the balance in time.

Guitar Center / Musician's Friend: Gear Card & Platinum Card

Same structure as the Sweetwater Card — a Synchrony-issued store card offering 0% promotional financing for 24 or 48 months on qualifying purchases, current promo windows running periodically throughout the year (check current dates before applying, they rotate). Also currently carrying a 34.99% purchase APR and 39.99% penalty APR if the promo balance isn't paid off in time.

Guitar Center and Musician's Friend are sister brands under the same parent company, so their card terms are functionally identical — the difference is just which storefront you're shopping.

Best for: Large purchases where you're confident about your payoff timeline and want access to the widest in-stock selection (Guitar Center's brick-and-mortar network plus Musician's Friend's catalog).

The Deferred Interest Trap, Explained

This is the part most comparison articles gloss over, and it matters more than any other line in this guide: 0% promotional financing on a store card is not the same as an interest-free purchase.

Here's how it actually works: when you finance $2,000 over 24 months at "0% interest," the card issuer calculates interest on that full $2,000 from day one — they just don't charge it to you, as long as you pay off the entire balance before the promo period ends. If you're even one payment short when the window closes, the deferred interest gets applied retroactively to the original amount, not just the remaining balance. At a 34.99% APR, that can mean hundreds of dollars in interest appearing on your next statement out of nowhere.

The no-credit-check, in-house plans (zZounds, AMS, Sweetwater's 3 Easy Payments) don't have this trap — they're genuinely interest-free with no deferred-interest structure, because they're not revolving credit accounts.

Final Recommendation

For most people financing gear under $1,000–$4,000: zZounds Pay As You Play or Sweetwater's 3 Easy Payments are the safer, simpler options. No credit check, no deferred-interest risk, fixed payments.

For a bigger purchase where you genuinely need a long runway (like a $3,000+ amp or custom guitar) and you're confident you can pay it off inside the promo window: the Sweetwater Card or Guitar Center/Musician's Friend Platinum Card can work — but set a calendar reminder for the promo end date, and treat that date as a hard deadline, not a suggestion.

FAQ

Does zZounds financing affect my credit score? Not for amounts under $1,000 (no credit check at all). Above that, they run a soft check that doesn't impact your score.

What happens if I miss a payment on a no-credit-check plan? Terms vary by retailer, but typically a late fee applies and future financing eligibility can be affected. These aren't reported to credit bureaus the way store cards are, but that doesn't mean there's no consequence — read the specific terms at checkout.

Is 0% APR financing ever actually risky? Only on revolving store cards with deferred interest (Sweetwater Card, Guitar Center/Musician's Friend cards). If you don't pay the full balance before the promo period ends, interest applies retroactively at a high rate. In-house payment plans without a credit card component don't carry this risk.

Can I combine financing with sale pricing or coupons? Usually not on the credit card promotional financing — most retailers exclude combined discounts on financed purchases. Check the current terms before checkout since this changes by promotion.

Which option is best if I have no credit history? zZounds (under $1,000) and AMS's shorter EzPay plans (4 or 6-pay) are the most accessible since they skip the credit check entirely.
