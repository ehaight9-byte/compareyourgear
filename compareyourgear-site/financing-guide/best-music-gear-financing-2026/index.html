Sweetwater vs. zZounds vs. Guitar Center vs. AMS: Which Financing Actually Saves You Money?

Updated October 9, 2026 with current Sweetwater and zZounds terms.

Quick verdict: For smaller purchases, zZounds' Play As You Pay and Sweetwater's Easy Payments split the cost into a few interest-free payments for a small flat fee. For big purchases, the Sweetwater Card's 24-, 36- and 48-month equal-payment plans are true 0% financing with no retroactive interest. The real trap is deferred-interest promotions (like Sweetwater's 6-month plan): miss the payoff deadline and interest is charged back to the day you bought.

The Two Categories of Financing

Before comparing specific retailers, it helps to know these plans split into two totally different products:

Store installment plans. zZounds Play As You Pay, AMS EzPay and Sweetwater Easy Payments split one order into a handful of payments with no interest. Most charge a small flat fee per plan, and each is tied to that single order: there's no revolving balance to manage.

Store credit cards with promotional financing. The Sweetwater Card and Guitar Center/Musician's Friend cards are real credit cards issued by Synchrony Bank. They need a credit check and open a revolving account. Their promotions come in two very different kinds, and the difference matters more than anything else in this guide (see the trap, explained below).

zZounds: Play As You Pay

zZounds splits an order into 4, 6, 8 or 12 payments, with 18 payments on select items, and never charges interest. Each plan has a one-time application fee: $6.95 for 4 payments, $13.95 for 6, $16.95 for 8, $24.95 for 12 and $29.95 for 18. The first payment is charged when your order ships, then about every 30 days. Plans cover up to $3,000 of gear, and zZounds may check your credit before shipping a partly paid order, deciding approval order by order.

Best for: pedals, guitars and amps up to $3,000 when you want a fixed schedule without opening a credit card.

American Musical Supply: EzPay

AMS offers 4, 6, 8, and 12-payment plans at 0% interest, billed directly (not through a third-party card) every 31 days after your order ships. The 4 and 6-pay tiers generally don't require a credit check; longer terms do. For bigger purchases (roughly $999–$17,500), AMS also partners with Affirm for an 18-month 0% APR plan, which does require a credit check.

Worth noting: AMS's customer reviews are mixed on customer service responsiveness compared to zZounds and Sweetwater, so it's worth reading current reviews on the retailer itself, separate from the financing terms.

Best for: Musicians who want a longer no-credit-check runway (up to 8 payments) than most store plans offer.

Sweetwater: Easy Payments vs. the Sweetwater Card

Easy Payments splits an order into 3 payments (orders of $100 or more) or 6 payments (orders of $400 or more), charged to a credit card you already have. Sweetwater adds a $10 fee on 3-payment plans or $15 on 6-payment plans, and your total Easy Payments balance can't go over $4,500. Applying doesn't involve a hard credit check, but you need a US credit card with enough available credit for each payment, and your card company's own interest applies if you carry that balance.

The Sweetwater Card is where the long plans live: 0% with equal monthly payments for 24 or 36 months on qualifying purchases of $349 or more, or 48 months on $449 or more, on select brands (some brands set higher minimums). Every brand also gets 6-month financing, but that one is deferred interest. Read our full breakdown: How Sweetwater Financing Works.

Best for: Easy Payments for mid-size orders if you already have a credit card; the card's equal-payment plans for big purchases where you want a long, truly interest-free runway.

Guitar Center / Musician's Friend: Gear Card & Platinum Card

Like the Sweetwater Card, these are Synchrony-issued store cards with promotional financing on qualifying purchases, with promo windows that rotate through the year. Before applying, check whether the promotion you want is deferred interest or equal monthly payments: they behave very differently if you don't finish paying on time.

Guitar Center and Musician's Friend are sister brands under the same parent company, so their card terms are functionally identical — the difference is just which storefront you're shopping.

Best for: Large purchases where you want access to the widest in-stock selection (Guitar Center's brick-and-mortar network plus Musician's Friend's catalog).

The Deferred Interest Trap, Explained

Store-card promotions come in two kinds, and they sound almost the same:

Deferred interest ("no interest if paid in full within 6 months"). Interest quietly builds from the day you buy. Pay the whole balance before the deadline and it's waived. Miss by even a dollar and the card charges all of that interest, going back to the purchase date. Sweetwater's 6-month plan works this way.

0% with equal monthly payments (Sweetwater's 24, 36 and 48-month plans). Your price is split evenly across the months and no interest builds at all. If a payment is late, you pay a late fee, but there's no retroactive interest bomb.

So the rule is simple: before you click "finance," find the words "deferred interest" or "equal payments" in the offer. If it's deferred, set a reminder to pay it off at least a month early.

Final Recommendation

For most people financing gear up to about $3,000: zZounds Play As You Pay or Sweetwater Easy Payments are the simplest options. Fixed payments, no interest, a small flat fee.

For a bigger purchase where you need a long runway: the Sweetwater Card's equal-payment plans (24, 36 or 48 months) are genuinely interest-free. Be much more careful with any deferred-interest offer, from any store.

FAQ

Does zZounds financing affect my credit score? zZounds may pull a credit report before shipping a partly paid order, and it says paying on time won't raise your score. It approves each order separately rather than giving you a credit limit.

What happens if I miss a payment on a store installment plan? You'll pay a late fee: up to $10 per missed installment at zZounds and $15 at Sweetwater Easy Payments. Past-due balances can be sent to collections and reported to credit bureaus, so treat these like any other bill.

Is 0% APR financing ever actually risky? Yes, when it's deferred interest. If you don't pay the full balance before the promo ends, interest is charged back to the purchase date. Equal-payment 0% plans don't work that way.

Can I combine financing with sale pricing or coupons? Usually not on the credit card promotional financing — most retailers exclude combined discounts on financed purchases. Check the current terms before checkout since this changes by promotion.

Which option is best if I have no credit history? Sweetwater's card needs a credit check, and Easy Payments needs a credit card you already have. zZounds Play As You Pay approves each order on its own and the shorter AMS EzPay plans usually skip the credit check, so those are the places to start.
