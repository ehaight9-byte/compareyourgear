Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit compareyourgear.com ("Gear Compare," "we," "us") and how it is used. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information collected automatically

Our site is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare. Like most web services, Cloudflare processes technical information about each visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of the request, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate traffic, such as how many people visited and which pages they read. Cloudflare states that its Web Analytics does not use cookies to track individual visitors.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site point to retailers such as zZounds and include a code that identifies us as the referrer. When you click one, the retailer may set a cookie in your browser so it can credit us if you make a purchase within its tracking window. That cookie is set and controlled by the retailer, under the retailer's own privacy policy. We receive a report of commissions earned, not your name, contact details or payment information.

Advertising

We may show ads from third-party advertising partners, such as Google AdSense or Mediavine. These partners may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure how ads perform.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without cookies, though retailers may not be able to credit a purchase to us.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, for example when we add a new advertising partner, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Gear Compare is an independent buying-guide site. We may earn a commission from qualifying purchases through links on this site, at no extra cost to you.
